Turn your clinical work into published research

CLINICTOPICS gives selected doctors free, hands-on support to design, write and publish their studies. If your project isn't selected, you can buy the same support in stages.

You stay the independent author. We help with the work around it.

Common questions

Is the support really free?

Yes, for selected projects. The support is provided in kind by CLINICTOPICS. There is no fee, and no cash is paid to you.

Do I need to have my ethics approval already?

No. You can apply with a synopsis. Ethics approval from your institution must be in place before any study work begins.

Will my idea be shared with anyone else?

No. Your application stays with CLINICTOPICS and its reviewers, under confidentiality. We do not send applications to companies or other sponsors.

Who is listed as an author?

You are. Authorship follows recognised criteria. Our writers are acknowledged for their help and are listed as authors only if they meet those criteria.

What if I'm not selected?

We'll tell you why. You can revise and resubmit, or buy the same support as a paid package. There is no pressure to do either.

What if I can't pay for a paid service?

Tell us. In some cases CLINICTOPICS issues an access token that waives the fee, or invites you into the support programme directly. If you have a token, enter it on the application form or when you request a review. A token affects payment and access only. It does not change how your work is reviewed. Read the token rules.

Can you guarantee publication?

No. Journals decide what they publish. We help you produce the strongest submission you can.

How it works

Every applicant goes through the same process.

  1. Submit your synopsis

    Complete the application with your details and a short synopsis of your study.

  2. Verification

    We check your registration and institution, and look for duplicate topics.

  3. Expert review

    Reviewers score your synopsis on scientific merit, feasibility, ethics readiness, relevance and our capacity. Reviewers declare any conflicts of interest.

  4. Decision

    You're told one of four outcomes: supported, offered a paid package, asked to revise and resubmit, or declined with a reason.

  5. Support agreement

    We agree in writing what we'll provide. The agreement confirms that support is in kind, that you keep control of your results, and how authorship and funding will be stated.

  6. Ethics approval

    Your institutional ethics committee approves the study before work begins. We help you prepare the submission.

  7. Guided delivery

    Protocol, statistics and data management, manuscript writing, journal selection and submission, and replies to reviewers. You sign off at each stage.

Timelines and what to expect

We aim to give you a decision within four weeks of the application closing for each round. Delivery time depends on your study design and your institution's ethics timetable. We'll agree a plan with you in the support agreement.

Token rules

How access tokens work, who can get one, and what they do not do.

An access token is a code issued by CLINICTOPICS. It lets a doctor skip payment for a paid review, or join the support programme without waiting for a selection round. Tokens exist so that cost is not a barrier to good research. They do not buy a better review.

The two kinds of token

Payment waiverSupport invitation
What it doesSkips payment for a paid reviewSkips the selection round of the support programme
Where you enter itOn the "Request a review" formOn the application form
What it coversAny review, Quick check only, or Expert review only, as stated when it is issuedOne support application
What still appliesThe review itself, done in the usual wayVerification of your registration, ethics approval, and the support agreement

The rules

  • Only CLINICTOPICS issues tokens, and every token is recorded with a reason when it is issued.
  • Each token has a set number of uses and may have an expiry date.
  • A token is issued to a named doctor or group. Do not share it, sell it or swap it.
  • A token has no cash value and cannot be exchanged for money.
  • A payment waiver works only for the review type stated on it.
  • A token stops working once it is used up, expired or revoked.
  • We may revoke a token at any time if it was issued in error or is misused.

What a token never does

  • It never changes how your manuscript is reviewed, or what the review finds.
  • Reviewers do not see whether a token was used.
  • It never skips ethics approval, registration checks, or the support agreement.
  • It never guarantees that a study will go ahead, or that a journal will accept a paper.
  • It is never linked to any product, company or outcome.

How to get a token

We do not sell or advertise tokens. If cost is a barrier, or you were invited to take part, tell us through the contact form. We consider each request on its own and cannot promise a token.

How to use a token

  1. Find the token field

    It is on the application form and on the "Request a review" form, marked optional.

  2. Enter your code

    Capital letters and spaces do not matter. A code looks like BYL-XXXX-XXXX.

  3. Check the confirmation

    If the token works, payment or selection is skipped and you see a confirmation. If not, the message tells you why: not recognised, used up, expired, revoked, or meant for a different kind of request.

Fairness and records

We keep a record of each token: who it was issued to, the reason, the dates, and when it was used. We use this to check that tokens are issued fairly and used as intended. This information is handled under our privacy statement. If a token is misused, we may revoke it and decline to issue further tokens.

The portal demo uses sample tokens (starting BYL-DEMO) that work only in the demo.

Pre-submission review

An expert read of your manuscript before a journal sees it.

Journals reject many papers for fixable reasons. A pre-submission review finds them first, so you send a stronger paper. You get a written report. You decide what to change, and you stay the author.

What you receive

  • A structured report on scientific soundness, methods and statistics
  • A check against the reporting guideline for your study type
  • A check of ethics, funding, conflict-of-interest and authorship statements
  • Suggestions for suitable journals and their author requirements
  • A readiness rating and a short list of must-fix items

Who it is for

  • Doctors about to submit a manuscript, whether or not it was prepared with CLINICTOPICS
  • First-time authors who want a second pair of expert eyes
  • Supported-programme doctors, who get a discounted first review

Need a statistician to look at your analysis? Ask us when you request a review. Supported-programme doctors receive a discount on their first review.

How it works

  1. Request a review

    Upload your manuscript, name your target journal and choose Quick check or Expert review.

  2. Payment

    Pay online, or enter an access token if you have one. Discounts apply automatically where they are due.

  3. Assignment

    A CLINICTOPICS editor checks your file. For Expert review, we match an external reviewer in your field, with no conflict of interest.

  4. Review

    The reviewer completes a structured form. Reviewers do not see your name.

  5. Your report

    The editor combines everything into one report, released to your portal. You revise, and you can ask for a re-review.

Confidentiality

Your unpublished manuscript is confidential. Reviewers agree in writing not to copy, share or reuse it. Reviewers are paid a fixed honorarium for completing a review, whatever the outcome, and never by any company. We will tell you before any software tool other than word processing and similarity checking is used on your manuscript.

Become a reviewer

We invite experienced clinicians and researchers to review by invitation. We ask for a short profile, a conflict-of-interest declaration, a confidentiality agreement, and one paid trial review. Reviewers receive a fixed fee for each completed review. To be considered, use the contact form.

Independence, ethics and authorship policy

  • Support is provided in kind. We pay no cash or gifts to applicants. Pre-submission reviewers receive a fixed honorarium for review work, never linked to an outcome or to a company.
  • We do not fund or select projects based on any product, brand or company.
  • The doctor controls the study design decisions, findings, conclusions and choice of journal.
  • Authorship follows recognised criteria. Contributions that don't meet them are acknowledged.
  • Studies proceed only after institutional ethics approval.
  • Selection uses the same published criteria for every applicant. CLINICTOPICS may also invite a doctor directly with an access token. Every token is logged with a reason, and a token never affects the scientific review or its outcome.
